# VIce Bartender — positioning

## Who it is for
Mac users (macOS 14+) whose menu bar is crowded with icons, especially MacBooks with a notch, and people who stayed on Ice and need it to keep working on macOS 27.

## Problem
Too many menu bar icons: the important ones get pushed out, the bar looks messy, and macOS offers no built-in way to hide or arrange them.

## Promise
For Mac users with a cluttered menu bar, VIce Bartender is the open-source menu bar manager that hides, arranges and searches your icons, unlike leaving the bar as macOS draws it.

## USPs (with proof)
1. Hidden and always-hidden sections, plus an Ice Bar that shows hidden items under the menu bar (README feature list).
2. macOS 27 support via Accessibility, with layout moves and Visible-row reordering (README; hidden rows are not reordered on the bar).
3. Open source, GPL-3.0, community-maintained fork of Ice (README, LICENSE). Free pricing: unverified.

## Tone
Plain, calm, practical; honest about limits (beta spacing, some features on the roadmap).

## Alternatives
| Alternative | Difference |
|---|---|
| Doing nothing (macOS default bar) | No hiding or arranging; items lost behind the notch |
| Bartender (commercial) | Closed source, paid; feature parity: unverified |
| Hidden Bar | Simpler hider; comparison with VIce's layout/search: unverified |
| Original Ice | Unmaintained; VIce continues it and adds macOS 27 support |
| Manually ⌘-dragging icons / quitting apps | Fiddly, no hidden sections, no search |
